Phase 1 · Data generation

Mixture sampling added after a geometric coverage bug was found in testing.

Phase 3 · Validation (the actual deliverable)
Aggregate metrics per parameter Reuse sbi_eval.py's report() — RMSE + calibration, called once per parameter. next
Recoverability map across the whole box Not just one test point — check for RB-blind corners (commuting-limit regions flagged in the literature scan) where the model would converge to a meaningless answer.
Bimodality / mode-recovery check Coverage alone won't catch a split (sign-ambiguous) posterior — cluster each test example's samples and flag when they separate into two groups. CONFIRMED 2026-09-19, not just a risk anymore: raw-feature check on all 10k runs shows corr(feature, |theta_J|) up to -0.65, but corr(feature, signed theta_J) ~0.02 -- the data encodes magnitude, not sign. This is exactly why the trained diffusion model's predictions collapsed near 0 (MSE-optimal mean of a symmetric +/- target) instead of tracking theta. Also found: model's posterior std (~0.08) was too tight even for a correct bimodal answer -- it hasn't learned to spread across +/- yet either. Also worth checking separately: draw_theta's shared box choice across all 3 thetas per draw may correlate |theta_J|,|theta_x|,|theta_y| with each other -- could confound magnitude-learning across axes. Full writeup: findings.html and index.html §5.
Worked-example posterior plots "Here's the input, here's the sampled cloud, here's the truth" — a few chosen cases, including one near the anomaly's own strength (θ≈0.4–0.7).
